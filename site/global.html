Right now · mood: risk-off

Money is split between crowded, expensive US tech and record-high gold — investors want growth but are hedging their bets. The thing to watch is whether it broadens toward cheaper markets.

The story behind each shift
  • 2011–12 risk-off
    ▲ Gold▲ US Treasuries▲ US dollar ▼ Europe▼ Emerging markets
    Euro-zone debt crisis. Gold hit then-record highs (~$1,900) as investors fled anything risky.
  • 2013 risk-off
    ▲ US equities▲ US dollar ▼ Emerging markets▼ Gold
    The 'taper tantrum' — the Fed hinted it would slow stimulus. Money rushed home to the US; the rupee crashed and gold fell hard.
  • 2014–15 risk-on
    ▲ US equities▲ US dollar ▼ Commodities▼ Emerging markets▼ Gold
    A strong-dollar US bull market. Oil collapsed from $100 to under $30, commodity exporters suffered, gold drifted lower.
  • 2016–17 risk-on
    ▲ Emerging markets▲ Commodities▲ Global equities ▼ Cash▼ US dollar
    Synchronised global growth — the rare year everything worked. Money spread out into EM and cyclicals; India rallied hard.
  • 2018 risk-off
    ▲ Cash▲ US dollar ▼ Emerging markets▼ Tech
    The Fed hiked four times and the US–China trade war began. Risk assets sold off; investors hid in cash and the dollar.
  • 2019 risk-on
    ▲ Equities▲ Gold ▼ Cash
    The Fed pivoted to cutting rates. Both stocks AND gold rose — falling rates lift gold by making cash less rewarding to hold.
  • 2020 mixed
    ▲ US big-tech▲ Gold▲ Everything (stimulus) ▼ Cash▼ Value stocks
    COVID crash, then a flood of central-bank money. A barbell year: money piled into US tech AND gold (a record ~$2,075).
  • 2021 risk-on
    ▲ Cyclicals▲ Commodities▲ Crypto ▼ Bonds▼ Safe havens
    Reopening and reflation. Money chased value, commodities and speculative assets; gold went sideways all year.
  • 2022 risk-off
    ▲ US dollar▲ Energy▲ Cash ▼ Tech▼ Bonds▼ Emerging markets
    The worst inflation in 40 years and the fastest Fed hikes in decades. Almost everything fell except the dollar and energy.
  • 2023 risk-on
    ▲ US mega-cap tech (the 'Magnificent 7')▲ Gold ▼ Small caps▼ China
    The AI boom. Leadership narrowed to a handful of US giants, while gold quietly climbed on rate-cut hopes.
  • 2024 mixed
    ▲ Gold▲ US tech▲ Japan ▼ Cash (as rate cuts began)▼ China
    Rate cuts began and central banks bought gold in record size. Gold hit fresh highs alongside US tech — a risk-and-safety barbell.
  • 2025–26 mixed
    ▲ Gold▲ Cheap markets / EM value▲ Broadening beyond US tech ▼ Over-concentrated US mega-caps
    Record gold on central-bank buying and de-dollarisation, plus early signs of money broadening out toward cheaper markets away from crowded US tech.
